# Changelog

## 0.1.2

- A character who has used all their movement is flagged as out of movement, with a red marker on the token and in the panel. Only a Dash or the next round clears the flag: undoing a move gives the movement back, but the character still can't move until they dash or the round ends. The GM can still move them.
- Optional support for Dxcufgb's Debug Recorder: when it is installed and the GM records, this module adds its state (round, phase and counts only, no names) and a trail of its main actions to the bug report file. Nothing changes without it.
